# Personal Blog Site

## What is this?
This is my personal blog site, where I will post articles, rants, and other random stuff. 

## Why I made this

<p>
    I actually had a <a href="https://goobusthenoobus.github.io/PersonalSite">former site↗</a>
    that I made at the beginning of Grade 9 - it was a bit messy and not nice to look at, though 
    I think some elements of it was cool, like the ASCII hero banner. However, my coding skills 
    grow very fast, especially as a beginner who codes like 67 hours a day. It no longer represented
    my coding skills. Imagine somebody looking at it, and being like "this guy sucks at making 
    websites!" It doesn't suck per se, but I do believe that it had fallen behind me, and I could
    make something better.
</p>

<p>
    Another reason I made this was because the <a href="https://crescent.hackclub.com/">Crescent YSWS 
    Program's↗</a> week 6 cards had one where you make a blog site, and I wanted a new personal site
    anyway, so why not combine them? I'm probably going to ship this to Crescent too btw.
</p>
